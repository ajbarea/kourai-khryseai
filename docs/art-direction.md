# Art Direction

Kourai Khryseai's visual and verbal identity draws from **Supergiant Games' Hades** — Jen Zee's painterly, mythologically-grounded character work — recast through the lens of Hephaestus' forge.

---

## Visual Identity

### Core Aesthetic

- Painterly, semi-realistic character work with expressive faces
- Warm color palette: golds, purples, greens, terracottas, with dramatic rim lighting
- Mythologically-grounded but accessible (not overwrought)
- Characters are **central** — they occupy significant portrait space
- Clothing and details reflect status, personality, craft (forge artisans, not warriors)

### What It Is NOT

- Anime-style (large eyes, simplified features, pastel color wash)
- Grimdark (no blood, suffering, or hellish aesthetics)
- Overly realistic (uncanny valley)
- Soulless generic fantasy (our characters have presence)

### Forge vs. Hades: Thematic Adaptation

| Aspect | Hades (Hellish Escape) | Kourai (Forge Craft) |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **Color Language** | Crimson, obsidian, bone | Gold, bronze, copper, forge-fire amber |
| **Character Framing** | Warriors, hunters, fighters | Artisans, makers, creators, servants of craft |
| **Emotional Arc** | Defiance, rebellion, freedom | Mastery, belonging, creation, collaboration |
| **Visual Motifs** | Underworld symbols, skull iconography | Anvils, hammers, fire, metalwork, tools |
| **Typography** | Serif, classical, austere | Serif + elegant, less "carved in stone" |

### Dialogue Frame Style

We follow the **Hades 1** dialogue frame style — warm cream text backgrounds, ornate gold borders with leaf/vine accents, classical warmth. This matches our forge theme far better than Hades 2's darker, angular metallic frames.

## The Forge Archetypes

Each agent embodies a role in the forge, with a distinct visual signature and personality color.

| Agent | Forge Role | Epithet | Visual Signature | Personality Color |
|-------|-----------|---------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Hephaestus** | Master Blacksmith | *Master of the Forge* | Golden light, forge-fire halo, scarred strength | Burnt sienna, gold, charcoal |
| **Techne** | Weaponsmith | *Artisan of Code* | Precise, confident posture, gleaming tools | Copper, teal accents, bright highlights |
| **Kallos** | Aesthete | *Eye of Elegance* | Elegant drape, artistic composition, soft glow | Violet, rose gold, silver |
| **Metis** | Strategist | *Architect of Intent* | Composed, measured, blueprint energy | Deep blue, gold, clean lines |
| **Dokimasia** | Assayer | *Guardian of Standards* | Rigorous, protective, precise measurement | Steel gray, bronze, amber |
| **Mneme** | Scribe | *Keeper of Memory* | Thoughtful, surrounded by symbols/text, warm light | Burgundy, bronze, parchment |

Epithets appear as subtitles beneath the agent's name in the dialogue box, following the Hades convention (e.g., "ATHENA — *Goddess of Wisdom*").

## Speech Tone

Agents speak in elevated, mythologically-resonant language while retaining personality autonomy. Think Hades' dialogue — archaic without being Shakespearean, mythological without being pretentious.

### What to Avoid

- **Modern slang:** "Yo," "gonna," "pretty sick," "no cap"
- **Corporate/generic:** "Let's touch base," "synergize," "best practices"
- **Condescending archaic:** "Thou shalt," "verily," "forsooth"
- **Overwrought:** Five-word sentences where two will do
- **Personality-erasing:** Forcing all agents to sound the same "ancient" way

### What to Embrace

- **Elevated vocabulary:** "I find myself intrigued" vs. "I'm interested"
- **Mythological references:** Natural to the world ("By Zeus," "as Athena would," "forge-bound")
- **Personality-appropriate elevation:** Each agent's voice still shines through
- **Concise archaic:** "I shall aid you" not "I do hereby promise to provide assistance"
- **Contextual formality:** Tone shifts with familiarity, not sentence structure

## Accessibility

No player should be excluded from the forge. Accessibility is not optional polish.

- All text meets **WCAG AA 4.5:1** contrast ratio
- Agent accent colors used for decoration only — never as sole information carrier
- Affinity and tier displays use text labels, not just color
- Font size adjustable (14px–24px), optional OpenDyslexic toggle
- All interactions keyboard-reachable, no time-limited inputs
- Typewriter text skippable with a single keypress
- All images have alt text, dialogue is sequential
